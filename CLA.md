## 1. Definitions

"Contribution" means any original work of authorship, including software code, styles, data, text, images and documentation,
that you Submit to us, including features, game modes and themes submitted through CausalQuest (CausalScribe or CausalArtist),
and changes proposed to our public repositories. "Submit" means to send to us in any form, including through the game, a pull
request, an issue or a comment, unless you clearly mark it "Not a Contribution". "Work" means CausalQuest, CausalData, our
public repositories and any product or service we provide.

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into a binding contract. You must not be located in, or ordinarily resident
in, a country or region subject to comprehensive U.S. sanctions, and you must not be named on any U.S. government list of
prohibited or restricted parties. If you make Contributions as part of your job, or your employer or anyone else has rights in
what you create, you must have their permission. If you contribute on behalf of an organisation, contact us at
contact@causalhub.ai before you contribute so that the organisation can sign a corporate contributor agreement.

## 6. Your promises

You promise that:

- (a) each Contribution is your original creation, or you have the right to Submit it under this Agreement, and you identify any

- (e) you have disclosed any use of AI tools in making it, as the manifest or pull request asks; and
- (f) the description you give is accurate and follows CausalQuest's honesty rules, for example never presenting a claim as

We are not required to use, publish or keep any Contribution. We may review, test, change, suspend, remove or decline any

rights that would prevent us from changing or removing a Contribution.

## 8. Rewards

We may award play points under the contribution rules published in the CausalQuest contribution repository, as they change from
time to time. Play points have no cash value. They are not wages, payment or consideration for employment, and cannot be bought,
sold, transferred, cashed out or converted into money, tokens or KRF (Knowledge Royalty Framework) credit. We may take back points
awarded for a Contribution that is removed for security. Apart from those points, you will not receive any payment, royalty or
revenue share for a Contribution unless we agree it in a separate written agreement.

## 9. Security

If you find a security problem, report it privately as our security policy (SECURITY.md) describes. Do not test against the live
game beyond your own Contribution, access other players' data, or disrupt the service.

## 10. No warranty from you

Apart from the promises in section 6, you provide your Contributions "as is", without warranties of any kind, and you are not
required to provide support.

## 11. Your information

We handle the information you give us as a contributor under the CausalQuest Privacy Policy.

## 12. Changes and ending

We may publish a new version of this Agreement with a new identifier. A new version applies only to Contributions you Submit after
accepting it. The licences you granted for earlier Contributions continue under the version you accepted. You may stop
contributing at any time; the licences already granted survive.

## 13. Law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. The CausalQuest Terms are incorporated into this Agreement, and
any dispute about this Agreement or your Contributions is resolved as the CausalQuest Terms provide.

This Agreement, together with the CausalQuest Terms and the published contribution rules, is the whole agreement between you and
us about Contributions. If any part is unenforceable, the rest still applies.
