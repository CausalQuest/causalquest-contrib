# Code of conduct

CausalQuest is built by people who disagree about causes and effects all the time. That is the point. Keep the disagreement
about the evidence and the work, not about each other.

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Do not share other people's private information.
- Do not use this repository to promote false health claims or anything that could cause harm.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.
Report a problem to **contact@causalhub.ai**.
